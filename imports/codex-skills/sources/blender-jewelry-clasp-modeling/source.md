# Ornamental clasp and connector modeling

## Establish the form

Read each reference separately for silhouette, construction and material. Identify the main frame, gemstone seat, connecting ring, ferrule and strap direction. Establish the whole pull path before adding decorative details.

Use front, side and oblique views to place the components. Ring radius, height and inclination form one spatial problem: a ring must connect through both openings while preserving the intended profile.

## Build the construction

- Give metal frames full cross-sections rather than a flat ribbon. Choose a round, oval, rounded-rectangle or tapered section according to its function.
- Gemstones occupy seated volumes. Set the frame and prongs around the stone's actual lower envelope.
- Model connector openings and the visible ring path in three dimensions. Check inner and outer edges in an oblique view.
- Flat-band ferrules should follow the strap width and pull direction, with a stable insertion segment.
- Smooth the free span of a cord while retaining rigid insertion blocks. Keep the silhouette and clearance at the connector.

## Refine an existing asset

Preserve accepted palette, grouping, materials, shape keys, weights and unrelated geometry. Change the specific form or connection requested. Reopen the saved copy and inspect the same views, including the rear/side connection when it is part of the task.
