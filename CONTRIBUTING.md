# Contributing to Chat Agent Harness

Thank you for considering a contribution to CAH.

CAH is licensed under the **MIT License**. By intentionally submitting a contribution to this repository (for example through a pull request), you:

1. certify that you have the legal right to submit the contribution; and
2. agree that your contribution may be distributed as part of CAH under the **MIT License**.

You retain your copyright in your contribution. If your employer, client, school, or another party may own rights in your contribution, obtain the necessary authorization before submitting it.

## Third-party material

Do not submit third-party code, assets, documentation, or other material unless you have the right to do so and its license is compatible with distribution in CAH. Clearly identify any third-party material and its license in the pull request.

## Pull requests

Pull requests should describe the change, the verification performed, and any relevant third-party licensing information.
