# Contributing to Chat Agent Harness

Thank you for considering a contribution to CAH.

CAH uses a dual-licensing model: the public project is licensed under **AGPL-3.0-only**, while the CAH copyright holder may separately offer commercial licenses. To preserve that model, contributions need an explicit inbound licensing grant.

## Contribution licensing

By intentionally submitting a contribution to this repository (for example through a pull request), you:

1. certify that you have the legal right to submit the contribution;
2. agree that the contribution is available to recipients of this repository under **AGPL-3.0-only**; and
3. grant the CAH copyright holder, **Tests4gptplay**, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable license to reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works from, distribute, sublicense, relicense, and commercially license your contribution as part of CAH, including under proprietary or other commercial terms.

You retain your copyright in your contribution. This grant does not transfer ownership of your contribution.

If your employer, client, school, or another party may own rights in your contribution, obtain the necessary authorization before submitting it.

## Third-party material

Do not submit third-party code, assets, documentation, or other material unless you have the right to do so and its license is compatible with CAH's licensing model. Clearly identify any third-party material and its license in the pull request.

A contribution that cannot be relicensed consistently with the CAH dual-licensing model may be declined or kept separate from the commercially licensed codebase.

## Pull requests

Pull requests should confirm that the contributor has read and accepts these contribution-licensing terms.

For substantial contributions or unusual ownership situations, the maintainer may require a separate written contributor agreement before merging.
